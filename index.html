Green Belt — attack & defence

Web & CWE belt: injection, boundaries, information leakage, authentication, cryptography, integrity, secrets, validation and authorization.
Mobile belt: the OWASP Mobile Top 10 — credentials, supply chain, auth, validation, communication, privacy, binary protections, misconfiguration, storage and cryptography.
First-try correct scores 100; finding it after a miss scores 50. Each belt shows a coverage map of its framework, including the categories it does not cover. Works offline in any browser.
Lesson content adapted from the OWASP SecureCodingDojo Green Belt (Apache-2.0); code excerpts rewritten and defence pairings authored for this pack. The mobile belt is adapted from the M4 SN50 "Own the Device" lab game; the OWASP Mobile Top 10 is OWASP's own framework.
